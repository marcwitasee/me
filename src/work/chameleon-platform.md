## The problem

Chameleon is a configurable cloud testbed that researchers at universities, national labs and companies use to run computer science experiments. It's funded by federal grants, so the platform has two sets of customers: the 15,000+ researchers who use it day to day, and the funders who have to see results to keep paying for it. Both needed to be served at once, alongside 60+ partner institutions building new features.

## My role

I led operations and strategic delivery for the platform. I was the technical owner for users and partners, and I was responsible for the reporting and proposals that kept the program funded.

## What I did

- **Redesigned onboarding.** I redesigned the onboarding process and automated parts of the workflow, cutting the onboarding cycle by about 40%.
- **Drove adoption.** I launched a quarterly webinar series (14+ sessions) and a monthly newsletter to help users adopt new features, including the platform's AI capabilities.
- **Rebuilt the documentation and feedback loops.** Redesigned docs and new customer feedback channels cut support requests by 15%.
- **Ran the funder relationship like account management.** I delivered quarterly executive reviews on platform performance and outcomes, and wrote 6 proposals. That work kept 5 grants totaling $14M active.
- **Kept partners aligned.** I built project plans covering scope, resourcing, timelines and KPI frameworks so technical teams and 60+ external partners agreed on milestones and success metrics.
- **Led an AI pilot** that added a RAG-based documentation assistant to the platform and helped make the case for a new $2.5M NSF grant for agentic AI workflows. See the [Chameleon Concierge case study](../chameleon-ai-docs/).

## Results

All 5 grants stayed active, new users got productive about 40% faster, and support requests fell 15%.
