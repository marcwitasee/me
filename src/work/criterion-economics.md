## The work

Criterion Economics, led by economist J. Gregory Sidak, provides expert economic testimony in complex litigation and regulatory proceedings. Its work covers patent damages and reasonable royalties, standard-essential patent licensing, antitrust and telecommunications regulation. Its clients are often large technology and telecom companies. Case details are confidential, so this page sticks to the public record.

## My role

I joined as an Analyst, was promoted to Senior Analyst in 2016 and then to Engagement Manager. My role grew from doing pieces of the analysis to shaping the testimony and running the analyst team behind it.

## What I did

- **Built the analysis.** At first I owned specific sections of a case's economic analysis: building datasets, running the models and drafting findings for damages and royalty valuations.
- **Structured testimony and economic arguments.** As I gained experience, I helped decide how expert reports and testimony should be organized, and how to position the economic arguments against the other side's experts.
- **Led the analyst team.** As an Engagement Manager I scoped the work on each matter, delegated it among staff, and kept the analysis consistent and on schedule for court deadlines.
- **Contributed to 34 expert reports, declarations and witness statements,** and prepared for the depositions and hearings that tested them.

## Matters I worked on

From the public record of the firm's testimony, 2014–2019:

- **Patents and standard-essential patents:** Qualcomm v. Apple at the U.S. International Trade Commission (two investigations, 2018). Netlist's memory-module investigations at the ITC (2017–2019). Saint Lawrence Communications v. Apple (E.D. Tex.). TransData's smart-meter patent litigation (W.D. Okla. and E.D. Tex.). ASSIA v. KPN in the District Court of The Hague.
- **Court-appointed neutral expert:** damages in Northgate Technologies v. Stryker (N.D. Ill., Judge Richard Posner).
- **Commercial and class-action litigation:** First Data v. SecurityMetrics (D. Md.). IDT v. Morgan Stanley (N.Y. Sup. Ct.). Three consumer class actions against Quicken Loans (Fla.). Lamar Central Outdoor v. City of Los Angeles.
- **Regulatory proceedings:** Verizon in the FCC's business data services proceeding. UPS before the Postal Regulatory Commission. Bell Canada before the CRTC.

## Results

Across five years I helped deliver the analysis behind 34 expert filings in federal and state courts, the ITC, three regulatory agencies and a Dutch court. Four of those matters went to live testimony. I learned to turn complex analysis into arguments that hold up under cross-examination, and to coordinate a team against hard deadlines. I carried both into running technical programs.
