## The work

Criterion Economics provides economic analysis and expert testimony in complex litigation. I worked with economists, attorneys and analysts on patent and antitrust cases for Fortune 500 clients, including damages and royalty valuations. Case details are confidential, so this page stays at a high level.

## My role

I joined as an Analyst, was promoted to Senior Analyst in 2016 and then to Engagement Manager, based on execution and client management.

## What I did

- **Managed engagements end to end,** from client intake to final delivery, and adapted the analysis as case requirements changed.
- **Built original economic models and datasets** and coordinated the analysis behind case strategy.
- **Contributed to 30+ expert reports and publications** used in litigation and business development, and helped prepare key testimony in federal cases worth $500M+.
- **Presented findings** to client legal teams.

## Results

Favorable outcomes in 24+ cases, including the Apple v. Qualcomm dispute.
