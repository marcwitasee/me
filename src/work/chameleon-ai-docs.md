## The problem

Chameleon serves 15,000+ researchers, and its documentation covers a lot of ground. Making that documentation easier to use was already a priority (the docs redesign cut support requests by 15%). An AI assistant grounded in the docs was the next step, and a way to show funders what AI could add to the platform.

## My role

I led the pilot and turned its results into a funding case.

## What I did

- **Built on retrieval-augmented generation.** The documentation was indexed for vector search, and a model served through Ollama answered questions from the retrieved passages, so answers stayed grounded in the docs.
- **Turned the pilot into a funding case** for expanding the platform's AI capabilities.

## Results

The pilot brought in $2.5M in new funding for the platform's AI features and contributed to a peer-reviewed paper at a top HPC conference.
